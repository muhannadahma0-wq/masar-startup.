# Twenty Specialist Roles

The configured model runs these twenty focused review tasks in parallel. They are roles in a graph, not twenty separately trained models.

1. Pitch structure and narrative
2. Problem and solution clarity
3. Value proposition and differentiation
4. Customer and user segmentation
5. Market sizing and assumptions
6. Competitor landscape
7. Business model and pricing
8. Go-to-market strategy
9. Traction and validation evidence
10. Financial model and unit economics
11. Funding ask and use of funds
12. Team capabilities and gaps
13. Product scope and roadmap
14. Technology and delivery feasibility
15. Risk and dependency review
16. Impact and outcomes
17. Claim and evidence review
18. Slide readability and story quality
19. Visual review of rendered PDF pages when available
20. Investor-readiness and founder action plan

Each role should cite relevant slides/pages or excerpts, identify missing evidence, avoid inventing facts or figures, and separate assumptions from validated information. The synthesis step removes duplicate findings, explains any readiness rubric, and leaves suggested edits for founder approval.
