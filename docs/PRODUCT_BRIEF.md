# Masar Startup — Product Brief

## Vision

**من فكرة إلى مشروع جاهز للنمو والتمويل.** Masar Startup helps founders review and improve their pitch, maintain a structured startup profile, find relevant support programs, understand eligibility, and prepare applications in one workflow.

## Initial audience

Palestinian founders first, with an architecture that can expand to other Arab markets. Never assume that an “international” call accepts Palestinian applicants without checking its official criteria.

## Product flow

1. Founder uploads a PDF/PPTX pitch deck or enters startup details manually.
2. Extraction and specialist analysis produce evidence-aware findings; unsupported figures stay unverified.
3. Founder reviews suggestions and approves any copy/profile changes.
4. Structured profile is evaluated against a reviewed opportunity catalogue.
5. Eligibility is returned as `ELIGIBLE`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, or `UNCERTAIN`, with the criterion, evidence, and official source.
6. Fit ranking is separate from mandatory eligibility rules.
7. Founder prepares documents, tracks deadlines and application status, and receives reminders when notification service is implemented.

## Core modules

Pitch analysis; pitch improvement; startup profile; readiness diagnostics; verified opportunity catalogue; deterministic eligibility engine; fit matching; application workspace; reviewer/admin source-verification tools.

## Non-goals for the first release

No promise of funding, no predictive win-probability claim, no fabricated market sizes or grant records, no automatic submission of applications, and no silent editing of founder profile or pitch content.

## Evaluation

Evaluate against human-reviewed pitch findings and eligibility labels. Track extraction coverage, false-eligible decisions, proportion of criteria with official evidence, reviewer agreement, and time saved preparing an application.
